What's Your Leadership Style?

Based on Northouse, Leadership: Theory and Practice, 10th ed., Chapters 8-11

This 12-question self-assessment maps your natural tendencies to four leadership approaches covered this week:

  • Transformational — inspires through vision and raises followers to higher levels of motivation (Ch. 8)
  • Authentic — leads from self-awareness, transparency, and an ethical core (Ch. 9)
  • Servant — puts followers' growth and well-being first (Ch. 10)
  • Adaptive — mobilizes people to navigate change and tough, unscripted challenges (Ch. 11)

There are no "right" answers — pick the response that feels most like how you actually lead (or would lead), not how you think you should answer. Takes about 5 minutes.

Your Dominant Style:

Growth tip:

Your profile across all four styles

Most leaders are a blend. A low score on a style isn't a weakness — it reflects where you naturally lean versus where you may need to stretch, depending on the situation (see Ch. 11 on adaptive challenges).
